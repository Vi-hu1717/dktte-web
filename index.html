About the company

DK Thermal Tech Engineering is a leading manufacturer of laboratory equipment. We specialize in manufacturing a wide range of high-quality equipment, including Deep Freezers, Incubator Machines, High-Pressure Autoclaves, Constant Temp Baths, Industrial Fermenters, and more. Under the esteemed leadership of our proprietor, Reghu, we have built a strong reputation for delivering reliable and efficient products. At DK Thermal Tech Engineering, we are committed to providing the best solutions to meet the evolving needs of our customers, earning their trust and satisfaction across the market.
